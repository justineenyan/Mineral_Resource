African Regional Macro Overview

Distribution of resources and export terminals grouped across the five African Union geographic pillars:

Northern Africa (7 States)
Heavy natural gas and crude export base (Sirte, Grand Erg, SUMED, Transmed, Medgaz) + OCP Phosphates (Morocco).
Western Africa (16 States)
Bauxite dominance (Guinea Boké >25B tons), Simandou iron ore, Niger Delta petroleum & WAGP gas corridor.
Central Africa (9 States)
Global battery nexus: DRC Cobalt (>70% world supply), Copperbelt, Gabon COMILOG manganese, Chad-Cameroon crude.
Eastern Africa (17 States)
Rovuma & Moatize coal/LNG corridors (Mozambique), Madagascar nickel/cobalt (Ambatovy), EACOP crude corridor, titanium.
Southern Africa (7 States)
Bushveld PGE complex, Richards Bay Coal (72 Mt), Saldanha Bay Iron (60 Mt), Jwaneng diamonds & Zambian copper.
